How FixIt works

What it does

  • Writes a summary or explanation of the text you paste, using Google Gemini.
  • Checks each answer against your request and suggests a fix. It never applies one without you.
  • Highlights every change, labels who made it, and keeps every version so you can undo.

What it can't do

  • It can't check facts outside your text, and the AI can still make mistakes. Read the answer before you use it.
  • Its checks are rules of thumb: "FixIt noticed" can be wrong, so you decide.
  • It doesn't save anything after you close the page.

Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + Enter Generate
  • Ctrl + Z Undo (outside text boxes)
